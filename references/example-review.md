# Example: Fair Market Value Review

This is a worked example showing the language, structure, and data presentation
for a standard fair market value review. Names and numbers are fictional.

## Example Provider
- **Name:** Provider 1
- **Specialty:** Pediatric Critical Care
- **YOE:** 8 years post-fellowship
- **FTE:** 1.0
- **Current base (post-annual-increase):** $310,000
- **Planned base:** $316,200 (2% annual increase)
- **Proposed base:** $325,000
- **wRVU:** 4,228

## Example Market Benchmarks (Blended TCC)
| Percentile | Blended TCC |
|-----------|-------------|
| 25th | $281,038 |
| 50th | $321,038 |
| 75th | $366,038 |
| 90th | $412,717 |

Sources: Survey 1 (n=89), Survey 3 (n=142), Survey 2 (n=34)

## Example Executive Summary
- Cohort: Pediatric Critical Care, 1 provider.
- Current base: $310,000. Planned base (post 2% increase): $316,200.
- Proposed base: $325,000.
- Incremental vs current: $15,000 (+4.8%).
- Incremental vs planned: $8,800 (+2.8%).
- Market positioning: proposed base at ~p52 (blended p50 = $321,038).

## Example Rationale (Consulting Tone)
1. **Internal equity:** Provider 1 has 8 YOE, placing them mid-career in the cohort.
   The proposed base maintains YOE-based progression without creating inversions.
2. **External anchoring:** Proposed base of $325,000 positions at approximately the
   blended p52, just above the market median (p50 = $321,038). This is appropriate
   for a mid-career provider being retained for continued clinical growth.
3. **Specialty floor:** Pediatric Critical Care benchmarks consistently above general
   pediatrician cohorts; the proposed rate reflects this specialty premium.
4. **Retention:** Targeted mid-career adjustment to address compression risk and
   align with market movement observed in the most recent survey cycle.

## Example Cost Impact
- Annualized incremental base cost vs current: $15,000
- Incremental beyond planned annual increases: $8,800
- Note: Fringe/benefits excluded. Apply organization-specific benefits rate for
  fully loaded cost. At 4.8% benefits rate: fully loaded ≈ $15,720.

## Example Governance Notes
- Proposed salary does not create senior-junior inversions within the cohort.
- All percentiles from governance reference (post-annual-increase file).
- Percentiles are NOT FTE-adjusted — part-time lower positioning is correct.
- Benchmark source: blended weighted-average across Survey 1, Survey 3, Survey 2.

## Language Patterns

### Good (empirical, neutral)
- "Proposed base positions at approximately the blended p52."
- "Incremental cost vs planned annual increase: $8,800 (+2.8%)."
- "Maintains YOE-based progression without inversions."

### Avoid (subjective, non-empirical)
- "Provider 1 deserves a raise."
- "This is a competitive offer."
- "We should match the market."
